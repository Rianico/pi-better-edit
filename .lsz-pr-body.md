## Summary

Adds a benchmark evidence record for read expansion. It captures a three-leg paired run on Pi 1.4.0 (`explicit-edit-benchmark`, 226 tasks) and states plainly what the numbers support: the 2.14.0 → 2.16.0 bump is cost-neutral and score-neutral once retry volume is normalised, and the ablation that appears to show expansion costing 7.7 % is a provider-error artifact.
